# PixiePlay

PixiePlay is a local FAQ and guided planning widget on the homepage and `/birthday-party-landing`. It is intentionally absent from `/birthday-party-bookings-vaughan` and the root of `birthdays.pixelpulseplay.ca`. It uses no AI provider, API key, external chat endpoint, or paid inference service.

The structure and topics follow the [shared birthday enquiry analysis](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ada028a-3a4c-83e9-88f1-8498a610817a): birthday planning, corporate/group events, existing bookings, and general questions. The reference is used as source material, not embedded or shown as the visitor's chat destination. Its suggestions for future automation do not create bookings or send messages.

The birthday planner collects a guest-count range, age range, optional preferred date, and standard/private preference. Visitors can explore before choosing a date. It keeps these preferences only in component memory, including when the chat is closed and reopened; starting over clears them. Continuing to the birthday enquiry passes any selected date and a short summary in URL parameters to prefill the existing form. Visitors must complete and submit that form themselves. Corporate/group-event answers link to https://events.pixelpulseplay.ca. Private-event replies and private birthday-plan requests use the same destination.

The birthday pages use party-focused launcher copy and a three-step planning action. The chat opens with a brief greeting and a question field. Booking links and up to three related questions appear only after a visitor asks about a relevant topic. Greetings and unrecognized questions do not trigger unrelated suggestion menus. Birthday questions can offer the guided planner as a next step. After eight seconds, a small invitation may appear once per browser session; it waits while visitors are typing, the tab is hidden, or another dialog is open. It can be dismissed and never opens the chat automatically. Only its shown/dismissed flag uses session storage; chat messages and planning preferences are not persisted. Opening the chat focuses its panel instead of opening the mobile keyboard. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

Answers use curated rules in `src/app/lib/pixiePlay.mjs`, customer-facing knowledge in `src/app/lib/pixiePlayAnswers.mjs`, and the existing website FAQ data. The knowledge includes all 55 questions from `pixel_pulse_chatbot_kb.csv` and the 20 supplemental questions from `Pixel_Pulse_Party_Chatbot_Answers.csv`; supplemental answers take precedence for duplicate questions. Exact questions and common paraphrases are checked before broad topic rules. Internal notes and historical quotes are excluded from the browser bundle. Unconfirmed supervision, spectator charges, toppings, equipment and admission policies remain subject to team confirmation. The widget cannot submit enquiries, check payment records, reserve dates or place holds; its replies direct visitors to the appropriate form or team. The owner confirmed birthday deposits of $100 for Pixel Punch and $200 for the other birthday packages, with e-transfers sent to connect@pixelpulseplay.ca. This policy overrides older draft deposit wording. Payment receipt and booking confirmation still require the team. Where the shared analysis flags other uncertain pricing, payment, timing, or promotion details, the guide directs visitors to the team/current package page. Current published birthday-page policies take precedence over conflicting reference suggestions. No customer names, email excerpts, or private enquiry details from the analysis are included.

Test with `node --test tests/pixieplay.test.mjs`, lint the changed files, and run the Next.js build. Browser checks should cover the uncluttered greeting, question-specific links and suggestions, birthday planner/back/reset, form prefilling, an unknown question, and mobile layout. Do not submit real enquiries during verification.
