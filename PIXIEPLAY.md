# PixiePlay

PixiePlay is a local FAQ and guided planning widget on the homepage and `/birthday-party-landing`. It is intentionally absent from `/birthday-party-bookings-vaughan` and the root of `birthdays.pixelpulseplay.ca`. It uses no AI provider, API key, external chat endpoint, or paid inference service.

The structure and topics follow the [shared birthday enquiry analysis](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ada028a-3a4c-83e9-88f1-8498a610817a): birthday planning, corporate/group events, existing bookings, and general questions. The reference is used as source material, not embedded or shown as the visitor's chat destination. Its suggestions for future automation do not create bookings or send messages.

The birthday planner collects a guest-count range, age range, optional preferred date, and standard/private preference. Visitors can explore before choosing a date. It keeps these preferences only in component memory, including when the chat is closed and reopened; starting over clears them. Continuing to the birthday enquiry passes any selected date and a short summary in URL parameters to prefill the existing form. Visitors must complete and submit that form themselves. Corporate/group-event answers link to https://events.pixelpulseplay.ca. Private-event replies and private birthday-plan requests use the same destination.

The birthday pages use party-focused launcher copy and a three-step planning action. Before questions or planning are available, the chat opens with a required name/email/phone form and an unchecked enquiry follow-up consent checkbox. The greeting and question field appear only after the private lead sheet confirms a successful save. After a relevant question, each answer shows one compact text link for that specific task. Food questions lead to food enquiries, waiver questions to signing/status, and booking questions to the appropriate birthday or events form. Up to two relevant follow-up questions are available behind a collapsed “More questions” control. The birthday planner starts only on an explicit planning request. Answers use larger text and the full message width so supporting actions stay secondary. Greetings and unrecognized questions do not trigger unrelated suggestion menus. Birthday questions can offer the guided planner as a next step. After eight seconds, a small invitation may appear once per browser session; it waits while visitors are typing, the tab is hidden, or another dialog is open. It can be dismissed and never opens the chat automatically. Session storage holds its shown/dismissed flag and an eight-hour lead-accepted expiry timestamp, so visitors do not repeat contact registration on every page reload. No contact details, chat messages or planning preferences are persisted in browser storage. Opening the chat focuses its panel instead of opening the mobile keyboard. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

Conversation actions and contextual follow-ups are selected by `src/app/lib/pixiePlayFlow.mjs`. Answers use curated rules in `src/app/lib/pixiePlay.mjs`, customer-facing knowledge in `src/app/lib/pixiePlayAnswers.mjs`, and the existing website FAQ data. The knowledge includes all 55 questions from `pixel_pulse_chatbot_kb.csv` and the 20 supplemental questions from `Pixel_Pulse_Party_Chatbot_Answers.csv`; supplemental answers take precedence for duplicate questions. Exact questions and common paraphrases are checked before broad topic rules. Internal notes and historical quotes are excluded from the browser bundle. Unconfirmed supervision, spectator charges, toppings, equipment and admission policies remain subject to team confirmation. The widget cannot submit enquiries, check payment records, reserve dates or place holds; its replies direct visitors to the appropriate form or team. The owner confirmed birthday deposits of $200 for all birthday packages, including Pixel Punch, with e-transfers sent to connect@pixelpulseplay.ca. The amount and transfer email are shared by the chatbot, public birthday pages/forms, enquiry auto-replies and new admin confirmation emails through `src/app/lib/birthdayPolicy.mjs`. Legacy birthday sheet deposit copy is normalized when loaded so old $100 amounts cannot be displayed by the website. The underlying workbook is unchanged. This policy overrides older draft deposit wording. Payment receipt and booking confirmation still require the team. Where the shared analysis flags other uncertain pricing, payment, timing, or promotion details, the guide directs visitors to the team/current package page. Current published birthday-page policies take precedence over conflicting reference suggestions. No customer names, email excerpts, or private enquiry details from the analysis are included.

Test with `node --test tests/pixieplay.test.mjs`, lint the changed files, and run the Next.js build. Browser checks should cover the uncluttered greeting, question-specific links and suggestions, birthday planner/back/reset, form prefilling, an unknown question, and mobile layout. Do not submit real enquiries during verification.


## Lead capture and analytics

Lead capture runs through `/api/pixieplay-leads` and writes only to the private `PixiePlay Leads` tab of the owner-provided “Chatbot - leads” spreadsheet. `src/app/lib/pixieLeadSheet.mjs` contains its exact spreadsheet ID. The public website-content/rewards workbook is never used for lead storage. The website service account has explicitly authorized Editor access; public link access was removed by the owner and verified. Each save checks Drive permissions and refuses public/domain-wide sheets. The tab holds submission ID, Toronto timestamp, name, email, phone, page, widget variant, explicit consent wording, and UTC timestamp. It contains no chat transcripts or promotion opt-in.

The API validates required fields, origin and request size; uses a honeypot and bounded per-instance IP rate limiting (eight submissions/ten minutes); requires server-verified Turnstile with the correct hostname; and returns success only after the sheet write completes. The existing GCP service-account credentials need Sheets access plus Drive metadata access to verify privacy. `RAW` value input prevents spreadsheet-formula execution. Submission IDs are reused on retries, and the existing PostgreSQL connection supplies a short transaction-scoped advisory lock across instances while checking the sheet for an existing ID. No lead/contact records or new tables are created in PostgreSQL. No emails are sent automatically. A sheet/verification failure keeps answers gated and provides retry/contact options.

The client gate is a lead-generation user flow, not authentication protecting the already-public website FAQs. After successful registration, starting over resets conversation/planning but keeps that browser session accepted. Closing/reopening before registration preserves the in-progress form in memory. The birthday invitation queues planning until registration succeeds. It remains absent from the birthday booking conversion page, per the existing placement rules.

`src/app/lib/pixiePlayAnalytics.mjs` only permits fixed event names and enumerated metadata: variant, topic category, destination category and error category. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, submission IDs, question text, planning dates and complete URLs are never sent by these tracking calls. Events cover open/close/reset, lead-form view, `generate_lead` after confirmed storage, save errors, questions, planner start/completion and link clicks. The existing live GTM container `GTM-53N567VP` was inspected and its GA4 destination `G-C6K82QG464` verified. Tracking uses `gtag` event commands targeted to that GA4 destination; when no global `gtag` exists, the standard command arguments are queued in `dataLayer` for the Google Tag installed through GTM. No additional GA4 forwarding tag is required, and these commands are not targeted to Google Ads. Configure `generate_lead` as a GA4 key event separately if desired; no Ads conversion settings were changed. Changing the website’s GA4 property requires updating this destination constant. Existing unrelated container tags are outside this helper’s control.

Verification: `node --experimental-vm-modules --test tests/*.test.mjs`, lint changed components/routes, production build, desktop/mobile gate and analytics checks. A clearly marked synthetic Google Sheet row was saved, retry-deduplicated and cleared without touching any existing rows. Browser form tests intercept submissions and use simulated Turnstile to avoid real leads or third-party analytics writes. The Google Chat/live-chat prototype remains removed.
